1. Who we are

  • Data controller: Andre Vieira (individual), Caucaia, CE, Brazil.
  • Data protection officer: Andre Vieira, engcomandre@gmail.com (Brazilian LGPD, art. 41).

2. What SorteAí does with your data

  • We do not ask you to sign up, we have no server and we do not receive your draws. Draws, titles, the names you type or import and the results record stay only on your device.
  • Importing a list: the app reads the text file you choose, on the device itself, and does not send it anywhere.
  • Narrator: reading aloud uses the voice installed on your device, without internet. No text leaves the device.
  • Google backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for the app: draws and names do not go to the cloud and are not restored on another device.
  • You can delete a whole draw (with its record) with the Delete button on the home screen, or delete everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The app is free and shows two kinds of ads: an app-open ad and a banner.
  • Ads are provided by Google (AdMob), which may process device data, such as the advertising ID, the IP address and technical information, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Google acts as the controller of that data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy.
  • We always request non-personalized ads: they do not use your browsing or usage history to target advertising.
  • Where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Google shows a consent form the first time you open the app. With no answer or if you decline, the app keeps working in full.
  • You can review or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy and ads.

5. Sharing and international transfer

We share data only with Google, through the ads SDK, as described above. Google may process that data in other countries. The safeguards it uses for those transfers are described in its policy (data transfers). SorteAí does not make any transfer on its own, because it does not receive your data.

6. Children and teenagers

SorteAí is not directed at children: it is intended for people aged 13 or older.

7. Your rights (LGPD, arts. 18 and 19)

You may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion, portability, information about sharing and withdrawal of consent, free of charge. Because the app keeps no data about you on its own server, almost everything is handled on the device itself (deleting the draw, reviewing consent). For any request: engcomandre@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the ANPD, the Brazilian data protection authority.

8. Security

The app does not send your draws off the device. Results are drawn with a cryptographic generator and the record is chained by hash so that nothing can be changed without leaving a trace. The app logs do not record names or consent data.

9. Changes and contact

Relevant changes will be announced in the app or on the store page, with what changed highlighted. Contact: engcomandre@gmail.com. Jurisdiction: the consumer's home court (Brazilian CDC, art. 101, I).

This text describes what the app does today. If the app changes what it does with data, this page changes in the same release.
